# Local SEO Monitoring Process
**For Growth Partner Clients**

## Purpose
The goal of monthly local SEO monitoring for Growth Partner clients is **not** to do advanced SEO work every month. Instead, it is to:

- Spot problems early
- Track whether the website + Google Business Profile work is actually moving the needle
- Give the client simple, useful insights every quarter
- Identify when bigger updates might be needed

This keeps the service valuable without requiring you to become a full-time SEO specialist.

---

## Tools (Start Simple & Cheap)

Recommended starting stack:

| Tool                        | Cost     | What It’s Used For                          | Priority |
|----------------------------|----------|---------------------------------------------|----------|
| Google Business Profile Insights | Free    | Views, searches, actions, photo views      | High    |
| Google Search Console      | Free    | Indexing issues, search performance        | High    |
| Google Analytics 4         | Free    | Website traffic from local searches        | Medium  |
| Manual Google Searches     | Free    | Checking local map pack rankings           | High    |
| BrightLocal (or similar)   | Paid    | Easier rank tracking + reporting           | Later   |

**Start with the free tools only.** Only add paid tools once you have consistent clients and want to save time.

## Monthly Monitoring Tasks (Growth Partner)

Do these once per month for each Growth client:

### 1. Google Business Profile Insights
- Log into their Google Business Profile.
- Check the last 30 days:
  - How many people viewed the profile?
  - How many people searched for the business name vs. category?
  - How many people requested directions or called?
  - Which photos got the most views?
- Note any big spikes or drops.

### 2. Map Pack / Local Ranking Check (Manual)
Every month, search for 5–8 of their most important local keywords on Google (from a non-logged-in browser or incognito window, ideally in their service area).

Examples:
- “plumber Chillicothe OH”
- “emergency plumber near me”
- “HVAC repair Chillicothe”

Record:
- Did they appear in the Map Pack (top 3)?
- Did their position improve or drop compared to last month?

**Tip:** Take screenshots and keep them in a simple client folder.

### 3. Google Search Console Check
- Look for any indexing errors or manual actions.
- Check which pages are getting impressions from local-related searches.

### 4. Quick Competitor Snapshot
Pick 2–3 direct competitors and quickly note:
- Are they ranking in the Map Pack for the same keywords?
- Do they have more reviews?
- Are they posting more frequently on their Google Business Profile?

Keep this very light — just a few notes.

---

## Quarterly Reporting (Growth Partner)

Every 3 months, put together a short report for the client that includes:

- Google Business Profile performance summary (views, searches, actions)
- Local ranking movement (Map Pack positions for key terms)
- Website traffic notes (if you have access to Google Analytics)
- Competitor observations
- 2–3 recommendations for the next quarter

You can keep this in a simple Google Doc or PDF. It doesn’t need to be fancy.

---

## Red Flags to Watch For

Contact the client proactively if you notice:
- Sudden drop in profile views or searches
- Loss of Map Pack visibility for multiple keywords
- Increase in competitor activity (new photos, more reviews, etc.)
- Technical issues showing up in Search Console

---

## Tools to Consider Later (When You Have More Clients)

Once you have 4–5+ Growth clients and want to save time, consider:
- **BrightLocal** – Excellent for local rank tracking and reporting
- **Local Falcon** – Great for checking how visible a business is across a map grid
- **SEMrush** or **Ahrefs** – More advanced (only if you want to go deeper)

---

## Realistic Time Commitment (Growth Partner)

- Monthly monitoring: 30–60 minutes per client
- Quarterly report: 45–90 minutes per client

This is manageable even when you’re first starting out.
