# Google Business Profile Management Process
**For Growth Partner & Signature Alliance Clients**

## Overview
Managing a client’s Google Business Profile (GBP) is one of the highest-ROI services we offer for local businesses. Done consistently, it directly impacts how often they show up in Google Maps and local search results.

---

## 1. Onboarding a New Client (One-time Setup)

When a new Growth or Signature client signs up:

### Step 1: Claim / Verify the Profile
- Ask the client if they already have a Google Business Profile.
- If they don’t have one:
  - Help them create one using their business name, address, and phone number.
  - Use their official business email if possible.
- If they have one but it’s unclaimed or not verified:
  - Guide them through the verification process (postcard, phone, or email).

### Step 2: Full Profile Optimization
Go through every section and optimize it:

- **Business Name** — Use the exact legal name (don’t stuff keywords).
- **Category** — Choose the most accurate primary category + up to 9 secondary categories.
- **Address & Service Area** — Make sure it’s correct. For service-area businesses, properly set the service area instead of a physical address if they don’t want customers coming to their location.
- **Phone Number & Website** — Use the main business number and link to their new website.
- **Business Hours** — Accurate and up to date (including holiday hours).
- **Services / Products** — Add all relevant services with descriptions (very important for local SEO).
- **Photos** — Upload 10–20 high-quality photos minimum:
  - Exterior
  - Interior
  - Team
  - Work examples / jobs
  - Products (if applicable)
- **Description** — Write a clear, keyword-rich but natural 750-character description.
- **Attributes** — Enable all relevant attributes (e.g., “Wheelchair accessible”, “Veteran-led”, “Women-owned”, etc.).

### Step 3: Review Management Setup
- Ask the client to forward review notifications to you (or give you access).
- Set up a simple system to respond to reviews within 24–48 hours.

---

## 2. Monthly Maintenance (Growth Partner)

For Growth Partner clients, perform these tasks every month:

- Post **2–4 updates** (photos + short text). Good post ideas:
  - Before/after photos of jobs
  - Team spotlights
  - Seasonal tips or promotions
  - New services or equipment
- Add **new photos** of recent work (aim for at least 5–10 new photos per month).
- Respond to all new reviews.
- Check for any profile issues or Google requests for more information.
- Update hours if they change.
- Update services/products if anything new was added.

**Time estimate:** 45–90 minutes per client per month (depending on how active they are).

---

## 3. Quarterly Tasks (Growth + Signature)

Every 3 months:

- Full profile audit (check for broken links, outdated info, missing categories, etc.).
- Competitor comparison (see what similar businesses in their area are doing well on their profiles).
- Review performance report (how many views, searches, actions, etc.).
- Suggest improvements to the client.

---

## 4. Tools & Access

Recommended tools:
- Google Business Profile dashboard (free)
- Google Business Profile mobile app (great for posting photos quickly)
- Canva (for creating simple post graphics)

Access needed from client:
- Full access to their Google Business Profile (you can request manager access)
- Google Business Profile Insights access

---

## 5. Reporting (Growth Partner)

Include a short monthly note in your client updates covering:
- New posts made
- Photos added
- Reviews received + responses
- Any major profile improvements
- Key insights from Google Business Profile (views, searches, directions requests)

---

## Notes for Signature Alliance Clients

For Signature clients, increase the level of service:
- More frequent posting (up to 8+ per month)
- More strategic content (tied to their local SEO goals)
- Detailed monthly reporting on GBP performance
- Proactive recommendations (e.g., “Your competitors are getting more photos — let’s do a photo shoot”)
