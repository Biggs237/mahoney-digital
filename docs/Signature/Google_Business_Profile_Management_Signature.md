# Google Business Profile Management – Signature Alliance

**Higher-touch, proactive management for premium clients.**

## Overview

Signature Alliance clients receive white-glove Google Business Profile management. This is not just posting occasionally — it is a strategic, ongoing effort to maximize visibility, reviews, and local authority.

You act as an extension of their marketing team, not just a service provider.

---

## 1. Onboarding (More Thorough than Growth)

For Signature clients, the onboarding is deeper and more strategic.

### Week 1–2: Deep Audit + Strategy
- Full competitive audit of top 3–5 local competitors’ Google Business Profiles.
- Detailed profile health report (photos, reviews, posts, categories, Q&A, etc.).
- Identify quick wins and long-term opportunities.
- Create a 90-day GBP strategy document tailored to their business goals.

### Week 2–4: Full Optimization
- Complete profile rebuild/optimization (more thorough than Growth tier).
- Professional photo strategy (recommend or help coordinate a photo shoot if needed).
- Build out Services, Products, and Attributes comprehensively.
- Set up review response templates and escalation process.
- Establish direct communication channel (phone/text as promised).

---

## 2. Monthly Execution (High-Touch)

Signature clients receive significantly more activity and strategy than Growth clients.

### Monthly Deliverables:
- **8+ high-quality posts** per month (strategically planned, not just filler).
- Consistent photo uploads (minimum 15–25 new photos/month recommended).
- Full review management + sentiment analysis.
- Monthly performance report with insights and recommendations.
- One proactive recommendation per month (e.g., “Your top competitor is dominating with video — let’s test short Reels”).

### Content Strategy
Posts should be tied to:
- Seasonal opportunities
- Service promotions
- Behind-the-scenes / team content
- Educational/value content for their audience
- Reputation management (highlighting 5-star reviews)

---

## 3. Quarterly Deep Work

Every quarter, deliver a more substantial review:

- Comprehensive GBP audit
- Competitor benchmarking report
- Strategy adjustments based on performance data
- Recommendations for the next 90 days

---

## 4. Annual Roadmap Session

Once per year (as part of Signature), conduct a full annual review:

- 12-month performance review of their Google Business Profile
- Competitive landscape analysis
- Create a 12–24 month GBP growth roadmap
- Present findings in a dedicated strategy call

This is a high-value touchpoint that justifies the premium pricing.

---

## 6. Reporting Expectations

Signature clients expect **detailed, insightful reporting** — not just activity logs.

Monthly report should include:
- Key metrics and trends
- What worked and what didn’t
- Competitive observations
- Specific recommendations for the next month

---

## 7. Direct Access & Communication

Signature clients get direct phone/text access during business hours.

Best practices:
- Set clear response time expectations (e.g., within 4 business hours).
- Use a dedicated business phone number or Google Voice (don’t give out your personal cell long-term).
- Document all major recommendations in writing (email or shared doc), even if discussed on a call.
