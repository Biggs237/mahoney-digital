# Signature Alliance – Service Delivery Overview

**Internal Playbook for Delivering White-Glove Service**

## Purpose of This Document

This guide provides a high-level overview of how to deliver the Signature Alliance offering. It connects the different service areas (website work, Google Business Profile, and local SEO) into one cohesive system.

Use this as your main reference when onboarding and managing Signature clients.

---

## 1. What Signature Alliance Clients Receive

Signature Alliance is our highest-touch, premium ongoing service. Clients in this tier should feel like they have a dedicated digital partner, not just a vendor.

### Core Inclusions (per Vision Document):

- Up to **8 hours** of custom updates, content creation, and optimizations per month
- Comprehensive **Google Business Profile** management + strategic execution
- Full **local SEO** execution with detailed monthly reporting
- **Monthly strategy & optimization calls** (45–60 minutes)
- **Direct phone/text access** during business hours
- **Proactive monthly recommendations** with prioritized action items
- **Annual full-site review** + 12–24 month improvement roadmap
- Content creation support (service pages, promotions, blog drafts when strategic)
- Direct access to the owner for escalations and high-stakes decisions

---

## 2. The Three Main Service Pillars

Signature work is divided into three interconnected areas:

### A. Website Management & Growth
- Regular content updates and optimizations
- New page/section creation
- Performance improvements
- Conversion rate optimization
- Content strategy support

### B. Google Business Profile Strategy & Execution
- Ongoing optimization and content
- Review management and reputation strategy
- Photo and media strategy
- Competitive positioning on Google

### C. Local SEO Execution
- On-page and technical local SEO improvements
- Keyword and content opportunity identification
- Competitor analysis
- Performance tracking and reporting

These three areas should be coordinated, not treated in isolation.

---

## 3. Service Cadence

### Monthly
- Up to 8 hours of combined website + GBP + SEO work
- 4–8+ strategic GBP posts
- Consistent photo and media uploads
- Monthly performance report
- One proactive recommendation report
- Monthly strategy call (45–60 min)

### Quarterly
- Deeper performance review
- Competitor benchmarking
- Strategy adjustments
- Website + GBP health audit

### Annually
- Full-site review
- 12–24 month Local SEO + Website roadmap presentation
- Strategic planning session

---

## 4. Communication Standards

Signature clients pay for **white-glove access**. Communication expectations are higher than Growth.

- **Response time**: Within 4 business hours during the week
- **Preferred channels**: Phone and text (in addition to email)
- **Availability**: Direct access during normal business hours
- **Documentation**: All major recommendations should be written down (email or shared doc), even if discussed on a call

**Pro Tip**: Use a dedicated business phone number or Google Voice for client communication.

---

## 5. Reporting Standards

Signature clients expect **insightful, strategic reporting** — not just activity logs.

### Monthly Report Should Include:
- Executive summary
- Google Business Profile performance + insights
- Local search visibility / ranking movement
- Website performance notes
- Competitor observations
- Clear, prioritized recommendations for the next 30 days

Reports should be sent **before** the monthly strategy call so the client can review them.

---

## 6. Onboarding Process (Signature)

Onboarding a Signature client should be more thorough than Growth:

1. **Deep Discovery Call** – Understand their business goals, challenges, and competitive landscape.
2. **Comprehensive Audit** – Full website audit + Google Business Profile audit + local SEO audit.
3. **90-Day Action Plan** – Present a prioritized plan for the first 3 months.
4. **Tool & Access Setup** – Get all necessary logins and permissions.
5. **Strategy Kickoff Call** – Align on goals, communication preferences, and success metrics.
