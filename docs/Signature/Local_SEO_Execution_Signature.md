# Local SEO Execution – Signature Alliance

**Comprehensive, proactive local SEO work for premium clients.**

## Purpose

In the Signature Alliance tier, you are not just “monitoring” local SEO — you are actively executing and optimizing it month after month.

This is the highest level of local search work you currently offer. It requires more time, more strategy, and more proactive recommendations than the Growth tier.

---

## 1. Scope of Work (Signature Level)

For Signature clients, local SEO work includes:

- Full Google Business Profile strategy & execution (see separate document)
- On-page local SEO improvements on their website
- Citation building and cleanup
- Review generation strategy
- Local keyword research and content recommendations
- Competitor analysis (ongoing)
- Monthly strategy calls focused on local search performance
- Detailed monthly reporting with insights and action items
- Annual Local SEO Roadmap

---

## 2. Monthly Execution

### Core Monthly Activities

1. **Google Business Profile Work** (see dedicated Signature GBP document)
2. **Website Local SEO Maintenance**
   - Monitor for technical issues (via Google Search Console)
   - Recommend or implement on-page improvements (titles, meta, headings, schema, etc.)
   - Suggest new content opportunities based on search demand
3. **Review Generation**
   - Help client implement systems to get more reviews
   - Track review velocity and sentiment
4. **Competitor Monitoring**
   - Track top 3–5 local competitors monthly
   - Note changes in their GBP activity, reviews, and local rankings
5. **Performance Reporting**
   - Create a detailed monthly report (see Reporting section below)

**Estimated time:** 4–8 hours per month (varies by client complexity and how much website work is needed).

## 3. Tools Recommended for Signature

You should move beyond purely free tools at this level for professionalism and efficiency.

### Recommended Stack:

| Category              | Tool                          | Notes                                      |
|-----------------------|-------------------------------|--------------------------------------------|
| GBP Management        | Google Business Profile       | Core platform                              |
| Rank Tracking         | BrightLocal or Local Falcon   | Grid-based local rank tracking             |

**Budget recommendation:** Budget $50–150/month per Signature client for tools if you scale (or absorb it into your pricing).

---

## 4. Monthly Reporting (Signature Standard)

Signature clients expect high-quality, insight-driven reports — not just data dumps.

### Recommended Monthly Report Structure:

1. **Executive Summary** (2–3 bullet points)
2. **Google Business Profile Performance**
   - Key metrics + month-over-month changes
   - Top performing photos/posts
3. **Local Search Visibility**
   - Map Pack / local ranking movement for priority keywords
   - Visibility score (if using a tool like Local Falcon)
4. **Website Performance**
   - Organic traffic from local searches
   - Key landing pages
5. **Competitor Insights**
   - What competitors are doing well
   - Opportunities for the client
6. **Recommendations**
   - 3–5 specific, prioritized action items for the next month
7. **Next Month Focus**

Send this report 3–5 days before your monthly strategy call so the client has time to review it.

---

## 5. Annual Local SEO Roadmap

One of the signature deliverables in this tier is the **Annual Local SEO Roadmap**.

This should be a dedicated 45–60 minute strategy session (usually done as part of the annual full-site review).

### Roadmap should include:

- 12-month performance review
- Competitive positioning analysis
- Local keyword opportunity analysis
- Recommended website improvements (on-page, content, technical)
- Google Business Profile growth plan
- Review acquisition strategy
- 12–24 month prioritized roadmap with estimated impact

This document becomes a major value driver and helps justify the higher monthly fee.

---

## 6. Communication & Access

Signature clients get direct phone/text access during business hours.

Best practices:
- Respond within 4 business hours during the week.
- Document important recommendations in writing (even if discussed on a call).
- Use a shared Google Drive/Notion workspace for all reports and recommendations.
