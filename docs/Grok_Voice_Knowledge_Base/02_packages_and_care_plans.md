# Packages and care plans — fact sheet

**Important:** Share **ranges** only on calls. Jeremy provides firm quotes in writing after understanding the business. Never invent a final custom price.

---

## Website packages

### Essential
- **Range:** $1,450 – $1,950
- **Most projects land:** about $1,650 – $1,750
- **Best for:** First real site, clean and simple
- **Includes (typical):**
  - Mobile-first professional design
  - Homepage + up to about 4 pages
  - Contact form and business details
  - Fast loading + basic SEO
  - About 30 days of edits after launch

### Growth
- **Range:** $2,650 – $3,450
- **Most projects land:** about $2,900 – $3,200
- **Best for:** Businesses that want more leads and stronger local presence
- **Includes (typical):**
  - Conversion-focused design
  - Homepage + about 6–8 pages
  - Strong messaging and lead forms
  - Dedicated service pages
  - Local SEO setup (service pages, meta, Google Business Profile help as scoped)
  - Analytics
  - About 60 days of edits after launch
- Often described as the **most popular** for lead generation

### Signature
- **Range:** $4,850 – $6,850
- **Most projects land:** about $5,400 – $6,200 (when discussed on site)
- **Best for:** Premium / high-trust work (e.g. remodeling), fully custom
- **Includes (typical):**
  - Fully custom design
  - Narrative projects / process style pages, custom scope
  - Deep strategy and custom work
  - Premium visuals and UX
  - About 90 days of edits + training

### Build timeline
- Most **Essential** and **Growth** projects: about **2–4 weeks**
- Signature can take longer because of custom design and content depth

### Domain and hosting
- Client **keeps their domain**
- Hosting is typically included with the project as Jeremy discusses with the client

### Bundling
- Website + ongoing support can save about **10–20%** on the project (when offered)

## Care plans (optional monthly)

### Essential Care
- **About:** $59 – $99 per month
- Security monitoring, backups, platform/security updates
- Small text changes (limited time per quarter)
- Uptime-style monitoring / basic status
- Email support (about 48-hour response)
- **Best for:** “Keep the site working.”

### Growth Partner
- **About:** $179 – $279 per month
- Everything in Essential Care style maintenance
- More monthly update time (up to about 3 hours)
- Google Business Profile help
- Local SEO monitoring
- Quarterly strategy call
- **Best for:** Consistent local visibility and ongoing improvements

### Signature Alliance
- **About:** $399+ per month
- Higher monthly hours (up to about 8)
- Fuller Google Business + local SEO execution
- Monthly strategy calls
- Direct phone access style partnership
- Annual planning / roadmap
- **Best for:** High-touch digital partner

Care plans are **optional** and generally **month-to-month** style. Do not invent lock-in terms.
