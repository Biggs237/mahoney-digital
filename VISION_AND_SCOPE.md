## Core Identity

**Mahoney Digital** is a digital agency that specifically serves small businesses who have been overlooked or underserved by larger agencies and generic web companies.

We focus on businesses that have weak or outdated online presences but have strong potential to benefit from a proper, modern website and ongoing digital support.

---

## Who We Serve

**Ideal Client:**
- Small, independent businesses (especially service-based businesses like contractors, plumbers, shade tree mechanics, trades, and local service providers)
- They often already have some kind of online presence (a basic website, Facebook page, or old listing), but it is not good — sometimes not even okay
- They are frequently ignored by bigger agencies because they are “too small”
- They have real potential to grow if they had a strong, functional digital presence

---

## What We Offer

### Website Modernization – Three Tiers

#### Tier 1: Essential

**Positioning:**  
A clean, fast, functional website that gets your business properly into the 21st century. No frills. No bloat. Just what you actually need to look professional and be reachable.

**One-Sentence Description (for website use):**  
The Essential package gives small businesses a clean, fast, mobile-friendly website with the fundamentals done right — professional appearance, easy contact options, and no unnecessary extras.

**Ideal Client:**
- Shade tree mechanics
- Contractors (plumbers, electricians, HVAC, etc.)
- Small service businesses that may already have a website, but it’s outdated, slow, or not mobile-friendly

**In Scope (What’s Included):**
- Mobile-first, responsive design (non-negotiable)
- Clean, professional layout
- Business name, contact information, and basic services overview
- Simple contact form
- Basic online scheduling (if needed)
- Fast loading times
- Basic on-page SEO foundations

**Out of Scope (What’s NOT Included):**
- Lots of photos or heavy image galleries
- Embedded videos, maps, calendars, or other heavy embedded elements
- Complex custom functionality
- E-commerce
- Advanced animations or heavy design elements
- Ongoing content creation

**Ideal Outcome:**  
The client has a fast, trustworthy, mobile-friendly website that makes them look legitimate and allows customers to easily find and contact them.

**Observable differences in the demo / real build (what a prospect sees on their phone):**  
- Narrow scope: homepage + up to 4 pages total (home, services, about, contact). No dedicated service pages.  
- Clean, functional layout with prominent click-to-call as the hero CTA. System sans, flat colors, narrow container for speed and simplicity.  
- Zero reviews or trust grids; minimal quick links. Footer and banner explicitly label "Essential tier example — 4 pages".  
- Sales framing: "exactly what many local trades need first — professional presence without extras."  
- Pairs with Essential Care (security + small quarterly updates only).

#### Tier 2: Growth

**Positioning:**  
A professional, well-designed website built for established small businesses that want to actively increase customer traffic and close more sales. It goes beyond the basics with stronger visuals, clearer messaging, and practical features that help the business grow.

**One-Sentence Description (for website use):**  
The Growth package delivers a polished, conversion-focused website designed to help established small businesses attract more customers and turn visitors into paying clients — with stronger visuals, clearer messaging, and practical features that drive real results.

**Ideal Client:**
- Established small businesses (contractors, service companies, professional practices, restaurants, etc.)
- They already have some online presence, but they know it’s not working hard enough for them
- They want a website that can meaningfully help them get more leads and sales

**In Scope (What’s Included):**
- Custom, professional design with noticeably better visual appeal than Essential
- Strong mobile experience with thoughtful user flow
- Clear messaging and service pages designed to convert
- Lead capture forms and calls-to-action throughout the site
- Higher-quality imagery and layout
- On-page SEO best practices
- Basic integrations (scheduling tools, Google Analytics, etc.)
- Faster performance and modern technical foundations

**Out of Scope (What’s NOT Included):**
- Heavy custom AI features or chatbots
- High-end custom graphics and complex animations
- Full custom development or highly complex functionality
- E-commerce (unless very simple)
- Large amounts of custom photography or video production

**Ongoing Services Differentiation:**
Growth tier clients are eligible for the **Growth Partner** ongoing support package (full details in the Ongoing Services + Bundling Model section below). This delivers the personable, proactive service referenced here — faster responses, quarterly strategy reviews, two major included website changes per year (beyond monthly hours), plus Google Business Profile management and local SEO monitoring.

**Ideal Outcome:**  
The client gets a website that looks professional, performs well, and is actively working to bring in more business, with a higher level of personal support than the Essential tier.

**Observable differences in the demo / real build (what a prospect sees on their phone):**  
- Homepage + 6–8 pages: dedicated service pages (e.g. AC, Heating, Maintenance) that prospects can tap into for problem/solution/CTA.  
- Service areas page (local SEO signal), reviews page (4–6 short local quotes), multiple varied CTAs (emergency phone, "Get estimate", areas links).  
- Brighter action-oriented color system (sky/cyan + amber accents), sticky professional header, Space Grotesk headings, stronger gradients on CTAs. Educational banner: "Growth tier example — dedicated service pages for SEO & conversions...".  
- Quick cards on home link deep into service pages; feels like a lead-gen machine vs brochure.  
- Pairs with Growth Partner (included updates + GBP/local SEO monitoring + quarterly strategy).

Clients who sign up for ongoing support at the same time as the website project receive the following discounts on the one-time build:

- Signature + Signature Alliance (12-month minimum): **15% off** the website (20% if the full year of ongoing is paid upfront)

This structure rewards commitment, improves project cash flow, and creates the recurring revenue foundation the business needs.

## Ongoing Services + Bundling Model

**Purpose:**  
Deliver genuine, ongoing value that keeps client websites working hard long after launch, while creating stable, predictable recurring revenue. This model is the backbone of a sustainable business that can reliably support family priorities.

### Pricing & Partnership Philosophy
- Ongoing support is priced to be realistic and accessible for the overlooked small businesses we serve (contractors, trades, local service providers), while remaining sustainable and profitable for us.
- Recurring work is not an upsell or afterthought. It is how we deliver real long-term results for clients and how we build reliable income.
- Bundling is a genuine win-win: clients lock in lower website pricing and guaranteed support; we secure predictable cash flow and long-term relationships.
- We are transparent about scope. Every package has clear "included" time and deliverables. Overages are discussed upfront at fair, discounted rates.
- No pressure tactics. Clients can start month-to-month after any minimum commitment, with simple cancellation terms.
- We do not sell "AI automation" or complex bots as part of ongoing work. Value comes from real human attention, smart updates, and practical growth work.

### Tiered Ongoing Support Packages

#### Essential Care
**For clients who purchased the Essential website tier.**

**Positioning (for proposals and website):**  
"Keep your new site secure, fast, and professional with simple, affordable ongoing care. No surprises."

**What's Included:**
- Monthly security monitoring, malware scans, and reliable automatic backups
- Core platform and security updates
- Up to 60 minutes of small content or text updates per quarter (e.g., price changes, new phone number, minor service description tweaks)
- Basic uptime and performance monitoring with email alerts if something breaks
- Email support with 48-hour target response time
- One quarterly status email summarizing site health and any recommended small actions

**Recommended Commitment:** Month-to-month or 6-month term (for small bundling incentive)  
**Pricing Philosophy:** Lowest barrier to entry. Designed so a shade-tree mechanic or small contractor can comfortably afford professional support without feeling it was a "luxury" purchase. Target range for most Essential sites: $59–$99/month.

**Differentiation:** Efficient and reactive. Focused on "keep it running right" with minimal time commitment from the client. No strategy calls or proactive outreach included.

---

#### Growth Partner
**For clients who purchased the Growth website tier.**

**Positioning (for proposals and website):**  
"A practical monthly partnership that keeps your website actively working to bring in more business — with real human attention and included updates."

**What's Included (builds on Essential Care):**
- Up to 3 hours of included updates, content refreshes, new sections, and small improvements per month
- Google Business Profile optimization and management (profile health, photos, 2–4 posts or updates per month)
- Monthly local SEO monitoring + simple competitor snapshot
- Quarterly 30-minute strategy review call (phone or video) — exactly as referenced in the Growth tier description
- Priority email support (24–48 hour target)
- Two major included updates per year beyond the monthly hours (e.g., new service page, significant content overhaul, layout adjustment) — exactly as referenced in the Growth tier
- Quarterly performance snapshot (traffic, leads, key pages)

**Recommended Commitment:** 6 or 12 months (required for full bundling discount)  
**Pricing Philosophy:** Reflects the noticeably higher level of included hands-on time, proactive local SEO work, and personal quarterly touch. Fair investment for established businesses that want their site to perform. Target range: $179–$279/month.

**Differentiation:** The first tier with real proactive elements and meaningful included time each month, plus the human relationship touch (quarterly calls) promised in the Growth website package.

---

#### Signature Alliance
**For clients who purchased the Signature website tier.**

**Positioning (for proposals and website):**  
"White-glove, high-access partnership. Your website has a dedicated advocate who treats its performance and your business growth as a personal priority."

**What's Included (builds on Growth Partner):**
- Up to 8 hours of custom updates, content creation, optimizations, and improvements per month (included)
- Comprehensive Google Business Profile + full local SEO execution, with detailed monthly reporting
- Monthly strategy and optimization call (45–60 minutes) — tailored frequency as referenced in Signature tier
- Direct priority access (phone/text during business hours for time-sensitive needs)
- Proactive monthly recommendations report with specific, prioritized action items
- Annual full-site review + forward-looking 12–24 month improvement roadmap session
- Unlimited minor updates within reason (clearly defined in the service agreement)
- Content creation support (service page refreshes, promotion pages, blog drafts when they serve goals)
- Direct access to the owner for escalations, big decisions, and high-stakes opportunities

**Recommended Commitment:** 12 months minimum to qualify for bundling discount; then flexible month-to-month or annual renewal  
**Pricing Philosophy:** Premium pricing that matches the highest-touch, highest-capacity, most personal service level. For clients who want an ongoing executive-level digital partner rather than a vendor. Target: $399+/month (scoped to actual needs).

**Differentiation:** Highest level of included capacity, proactive leadership, personal relationship, and direct access — matching the white-glove promise in the Signature website tier.

### Bundling Discounts & Incentives

When a client commits to an ongoing support package **at the same time they sign the website project**, they receive a meaningful, transparent discount on the one-time website build:

- **Essential website + Essential Care** (6-month minimum commitment): 10% discount on the website project
- **Growth website + Growth Partner** (6-month minimum commitment): 15% discount on the website project
- **Signature website + Signature Alliance** (12-month commitment): 15% discount on the website project; 20% if paid annually upfront for the ongoing package

Additional incentives that apply across tiers:
- Pay 12 months of ongoing support upfront at the time of the website project → extra 10% discount on the ongoing package itself
- Bundle multiple ongoing services (e.g., full maintenance + dedicated local SEO as separate tracked items) → additional 5% on each component

**Why this structure exists:** It rewards clients who are serious about long-term results, improves project cash flow on the build side, and creates the recurring revenue foundation that makes the entire business sustainable and able to support family.

### General Terms & Guardrails (All Tiers)
- Clear, written scope of "included" work is defined in a simple one-page service agreement before any work begins.
- Work beyond included hours is billed at a pre-agreed discounted hourly rate (rate decreases with higher tier commitment).
- 30 days' written notice to pause, reduce scope, or cancel after the minimum term.
- All packages include the non-negotiable core "Keep It Running" elements: security monitoring, automatic backups, and critical updates.
- No hidden fees. Every deliverable and time allowance is discussed and agreed in plain language before the client signs.
- We reserve the right to decline ongoing work that falls outside our focused service model or that would require us to violate our anti-goals.
