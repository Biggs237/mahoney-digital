A practice built on individual needs

Since 1983, Musser & McGinnis Physical Therapy has focused on one thing: serving the unique needs of each patient who walks through the door.

What started as William Musser’s private practice has continued under the ownership of Matthew A. McGinnis, DPT. We keep the team small on purpose so you get consistent, attentive care instead of being passed between providers.

Whether you’re recovering from surgery, managing chronic pain, returning from a work injury, or simply want to move better, we build a plan around your goals.
