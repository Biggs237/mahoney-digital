Optional add-on

Missed-call recovery

When a call is missed, the system can automatically text the number, capture what they need, and notify the owner so the lead doesn’t disappear.

1. Customer receives this text

Text from Summit Comfort HVAC

Sorry we missed your call. Reply with your name and what you need (AC, heating, tune-up) and we’ll get back to you quickly.

2. Customer replies

Mike — AC not cooling. 123 Oak St, Chillicothe

3. Owner gets this notification

New lead from missed call

Mike · AC not cooling · 123 Oak St, Chillicothe
Phone: 740-555-0199

This runs automatically. The website package price stays the same whether you add it or not.
