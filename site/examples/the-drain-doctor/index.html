Services

Main sewer and water line replacement and repair, plus the day-to-day plumbing work Chillicothe homes and restaurants actually need.

Main sewer installations

Signs of a broken sewer pipe can be as subtle as a gurgling toilet or as noticeable as a flooded yard. Mike is well-versed in sewer layouts, regardless of your property.

Water line installation

Whether it’s old age or freeze–thaw damage, water line installation is done to code, without leaks, and chemical resistant.

Drain cleaning

Skip harsh store-bought chemicals. Fast response and effective results on clogged drains.

Plumbing fixtures

Fixture installs — bring your own, or talk through what fits your home.

Clean outs

Yard clean outs so a toilet doesn’t have to be pulled to run a snake. 4-inch PVC cap at grade for future access without a big machine in the house.

Sump pumps

New sump pump installs, including cutting through concrete, setting the canister, and tying into the 4-inch PVC main.

Gate valves

Worn gate or shut-off valves that hurt water pressure — including the main coming into the home — replaced with a ¾-inch brass ball valve when that’s what’s needed.

Leaky toilets

Unattended leaks rot floors. Cut-out, re-pipe, new flooring, and a toilet set back on solid work.

Deep sewer repair

When a sewer sits nine feet down, you need someone who will actually dig it, not guess from the surface.

Restaurant grease drains

Grease drains that are almost completely clogged — opened up so the kitchen can run again.

Hundred-foot sewer line

A hundred-foot sewer from the house out to a brand-new septic. Three feet off the house, an unknown 1,500-gallon tank — probably 65 years old — showed up in the bucket. The job had to work around it, and the price had to go up. That’s how it goes when nobody knew it was there.

Clean out installation

Dig the line, cut a section, and build a 4-inch PVC clean out from the bottom of the trench up to grade. Later, nobody has to drag a big sewer machine through the house or pull a toilet to snake the line.

Sump pump installation

Started with a flat slab — no hole. Cut the circle, set the canister, installed the pump, re-laid the concrete, and ran the discharge into the 4-inch PVC main.

Gate valves & leaky toilets

Inside of a gate valve that will wreck water pressure — often the main shut-off, and often due for a ¾-inch brass ball valve.
