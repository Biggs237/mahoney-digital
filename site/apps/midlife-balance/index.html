Midlife Balance

A private space to notice how you feel in midlife — check-ins, gentle patterns, and visit prep. Data stays on your phone.

What it is

Midlife Balance helps women (and anyone tracking similar symptoms) log how days feel, spot personal patterns over time, and prepare for medical appointments with a private summary.

  • Daily symptom check-ins (severity scale you choose)
  • History and simple patterns (last 14 days)
  • Visit prep talking points from your own logs
  • Optional PIN / fingerprint / face app lock
  • Encrypted storage on the device · no account required

What it is not

Not a medical device. Not for diagnosis or treatment. Wellness tracking only — not medical advice.
