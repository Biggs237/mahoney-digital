Privacy Policy

MileMark Android app · Effective August 7, 2026 · Last updated August 7, 2026

This policy describes how the MileMark mobile application (“the App”), published by Mahoney Digital (“we,” “us”), handles information. The App is designed so your vehicle and service records stay on your device for core use.

1. Who we are

Mahoney Digital
Website: https://mahoneydigital.net
App page: https://mahoneydigital.net/apps/milemark/
Support email: hello@mahoneydigital.net

2. What the App does

MileMark is a personal vehicle service log. You can record vehicles, log maintenance services, set simple reminder intervals, and (optionally) receive local notifications when work may be due. You may export or share a text history from the device if that feature is enabled in your build.

The App is a personal record-keeping tool only. Interval templates are common starting points, not manufacturer schedules for your exact vehicle. The App does not diagnose vehicles, read diagnostic codes, or provide repair advice.

3. Information the App stores on your device

If you use the App, it may store on your device:

  • Vehicle details you enter (for example nickname, year, make, model, plate, VIN, odometer reading)
  • Whether you marked odometer as unused or broken for a vehicle
  • Service log entries (type, date, odometer, cost, shop or DIY flag, notes)
  • Interval and reminder settings you choose
  • App settings (for example notifications on/off, selected vehicle, premium unlock state if present)
  • Local notification scheduling data used by the operating system

We design the App so these items stay in private app storage on your phone or tablet and are not uploaded to Mahoney Digital as part of normal core use in the current version.

4. What we do not do by default

  • We do not require you to create an account with us to use core features.
  • We do not operate a cloud sync service for your vehicles or service history in the current version.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not include advertising SDKs in the App to track you across other apps.
  • We do not use your service history for advertising.

5. How data is protected on the device

  • Core data is stored in the App’s private storage on your device (for example Android DataStore / app-private preferences).
  • Protection also depends on your device lock screen, OS updates, and whether you grant others access to your phone.
  • If you uninstall the App, the operating system typically deletes the App’s private storage. Export or back up anything you want to keep before uninstalling.

6. Permissions

Depending on version and your choices, the App may request:

  • Notifications — only if you enable due or odometer reminders, so the system can show local alerts on your device.

The App does not need an internet connection for core logging and due-date features in the current version. If a future feature uses network access, we will update this policy.

7. Sharing you initiate

If you use export or the Android share sheet, you choose what leaves the device and where it goes (for example email, messaging, or another app). That destination’s privacy practices apply once the content leaves MileMark.

8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
