MileMark

Know what you did to the car, what’s due next, and keep a clean history when you sell. Data stays on your phone. No account required.

What it is

MileMark is a personal multi-vehicle service log and reminder app. Log oil changes, rotations, and other work; set simple intervals; see what’s due soon or overdue; and export history when you need it.

  • Multi-vehicle garage (one phone, several cars or a work truck)
  • Service log: type, date, odometer, cost, DIY or shop, notes
  • Due tracking by miles and/or months (templates you can treat as starting points)
  • Optional local reminders when something is due soon or overdue
  • Support for a broken or unused odometer — time-based due dates only
  • Stored on your device · no account required for core use

What it is not

MileMark is a personal maintenance notebook, not a mechanic and not a diagnostic tool. Interval templates are common starting points only — confirm work with your owner’s manual or a qualified shop. The app does not read OBD codes, diagnose problems, or provide repair advice.
