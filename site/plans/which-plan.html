Which Plan Is Right For You?

Not sure which ongoing support plan fits your business? Answer these quick questions to find the best fit.

1. How involved do you want to be with your website?

A. I want it to stay secure and professional with minimal effort.
→ Essential Care is the best fit.

B. I want regular updates and help generating more leads locally.
→ Growth Partner is likely the right choice.

C. I want a true strategic partner who’s deeply involved in my online growth.
→ Signature Alliance is designed for you.
2. How important is Google Business Profile and local visibility to your business?

A. Not a priority right now.
→ Essential Care

B. Important — I want consistent management and monitoring.
→ Growth Partner

C. Very important — I want proactive strategy and full execution.
→ Signature Alliance
3. How much communication and strategy support do you want?

A. Just occasional updates via email.
→ Essential Care

B. Quarterly check-ins and planning.
→ Growth Partner

C. Monthly strategy calls and direct access when needed.
→ Signature Alliance

Quick Summary

Essential Care — Simple, affordable maintenance. Perfect if you just want your site to stay secure and up-to-date with very little involvement.

Growth Partner — The sweet spot for most established businesses. Regular updates, Google Business Profile management, local SEO monitoring, and quarterly strategy.

Signature Alliance — Premium white-glove service. Maximum hours, direct access, monthly strategy, and long-term planning. For businesses that want a true ongoing digital partner.
