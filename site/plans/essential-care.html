Who This Is For

Essential Care is designed for small businesses that want their new website to stay secure and functional without needing regular updates or strategy work. It’s ideal for:

  • Contractors, mechanics, and service businesses with basic websites
  • Clients who want “set it and forget it” peace of mind
  • Businesses that don’t need ongoing content changes or marketing support

What’s Included

Core Maintenance

  • Monthly security monitoring and malware scans
  • Reliable automatic backups
  • Regular core platform and security updates
  • Uptime monitoring with alerts if something goes wrong

Support & Updates

  • Up to 60 minutes of small text or content changes per quarter (price updates, phone number changes, minor service description tweaks, etc.)
  • Email support with a 48-hour response target

Quarterly Check-In

  • One simple quarterly status email summarizing your site’s health and performance

What’s Not Included

  • Major website updates or new pages
  • Regular content creation or blog posts
  • Google Business Profile management
  • Local SEO work or reporting
  • Strategy calls or proactive recommendations
  • Priority or phone support
