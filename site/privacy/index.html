Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

Mahoney Digital (“we”, “us”, or “our”) operates the website mahoneydigital.net. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit our site or contact us.

Information We Collect

When you submit the contact or quote form, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Business name
  • Phone number and/or email address
  • The message or details you provide about what you need

We also automatically collect certain technical information through cookies and similar technologies, including the Meta Pixel. This may include your IP address, browser type, pages visited, and interactions on the site.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your quote or contact request
  • Provide website, care plan, or AI helper services
  • Improve our website and services

We use the Meta Pixel and related tools to measure the effectiveness of our advertising, understand how visitors use the site, and deliver relevant ads.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information with:

  • Meta (Facebook/Instagram) through the Meta Pixel for advertising and analytics purposes
  • Service providers who help us operate the website or deliver services (for example, hosting or form handling)

We only share what is necessary.

Your Choices

You can contact us at any time to:

  • Request a copy of the information we have about you
  • Ask us to update or delete your information
  • Opt out of marketing communications

For ad preferences related to Meta, you can manage settings through your Facebook or Instagram account or visit Meta’s ad preferences tools.

Data Retention

We keep contact form submissions only as long as needed to respond to your request and provide services, unless a longer period is required by law.

Contact Us
