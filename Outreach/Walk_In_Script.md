# Walk-in script (business card)

**Use when:** Cards arrive · local business nearby · no appointment  
**From:** Jeremy Mahoney · **Card / site:** mahoneydigital.net · **(740) 530-8790**

---

## 30-second version

> “Hi — I’m Jeremy, I work from right over here. I help small local businesses get a simple website that works on a phone — affordable one-time build, usually around sixteen to seventeen hundred for most shops, not three or four thousand like the big agencies. If you ever want updates handled for you, I do that too for a small monthly fee. Here’s my card — totally no pressure. If it’s useful, I can show you a quick example on my phone in ten seconds.”

---

## If they ask “what do you do?”

> “Fast, mobile-first sites for contractors, mechanics, lawn care, trades — honest pricing, no long contract. I also offer optional care plans so security and small text changes aren’t on your plate.”

---

## If they say “we already have a site”

> “That’s great — a lot of folks I talk to do, but it’s slow on a phone or doesn’t bring calls. I only suggest changes if I see something real. Happy to take a quick look if you want — no charge for a ten-minute opinion.”

---

## If they say “how much?”

> “Most straightforward sites land around **$1,650–$1,750** one-time. I’ll put it in writing before you pay anything. Monthly updates are optional — usually **under a hundred a month** if you want hands-off.”
