## How to Use This File

1. **Add a row** when a new lead enters the pipeline — even if it's just a name and source.
2. **Update `Last Touch`** every time you email, call, or meet.
3. **Set `Next Action`** with a specific date so nothing goes stale.
4. **Move to Lost or Nurture** with a note — don't delete rows; the history informs your outreach.
5. **Review weekly** — anything in `Contacted` for 7+ days without a reply gets a follow-up.
