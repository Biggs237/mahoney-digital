# Outreach Email Templates

Copy-paste templates for cold and warm outreach. Personalize the bracketed fields — generic emails get ignored.

**Rules:**
- Keep it under 150 words
- Lead with their problem, not your credentials
- One clear call to action
- No attachments on first touch

---

## Template 1 — Cold Outreach (Outdated Website)

**Subject:** Quick thought on [Business Name]'s website

---

Would a 15-minute call next week be worth it to see if there's a quick win? No obligation.

## Personalization Checklist

Before sending, confirm:

- [ ] Business name spelled correctly
- [ ] At least one specific detail about their business (review, location, service, referral)
- [ ] Subject line references their business, not yours
- [ ] CTA is one action (call, reply, or "send summary")
- [ ] Under 150 words
- [ ] Logged in [leads/leads-tracker.md](../leads/leads-tracker.md)
