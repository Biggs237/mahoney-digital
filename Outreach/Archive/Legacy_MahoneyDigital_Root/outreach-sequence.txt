# Outreach Sequence - Mahoney Digital

**Goal:** Consistent, low-pressure follow-up that builds trust.

1. **Day 0 - Initial Email**  
   Value-first message highlighting a specific problem.

2. **Day 4 - Follow-up**  
   "Just circling back on my previous email..."

3. **Day 8 - X/LinkedIn DM**  
   Short and personal.

4. **Day 12 - Final Value Add**  
   Offer a free mini-audit or tip.

Keep tone helpful and local. Track everything in leads-tracker.md.
